Mera website tour robot abhi sirf har page ke hero section ko explain karke 
ruk ja raha hai. Mujhe chahiye ki wo puri website ke har section, har page, 
aur har important detail ko cover kare. Is tarah fix karo:

1. Sabse pehle, puri website ka ek complete "Tour Map" banao — har page ke 
   har section ko list karo (na sirf hero). Jaise example ke liye:

   HOME PAGE:
   - Hero/Banner section
   - About School intro (short)
   - Why Choose Us / Features
   - Courses/Programs offered
   - Chairman/Director message
   - Achievements/Stats (student count, years, results)
   - Testimonials
   - Gallery preview
   - Latest Blog/News
   - Contact/Admission CTA
   - Footer (address, social links, contact info)

   ABOUT US PAGE:
   - School history
   - Vision & Mission
   - Infrastructure/facilities
   - Faculty/Leadership team

   COURSES PAGE:
   - Har course ka naam, duration, aur short description alag-alag step me

   BRANCHES PAGE:
   - Har branch ki location aur details

   GALLERY PAGE:
   - Photos/videos overview

   CONTACT PAGE:
   - Address, phone, email, map location, enquiry form

   (Mujhe apni actual website ke real sections ke hisaab se ye list update 
   karni hai — upar sirf example hai)

   HAR SECTION ka apna alag step ho, sirf hero ka ek step nahi. Format:

3. Agar website multi-page hai (alag-alag URLs par pages), to "Next" click 
   karne par agar agla step doosre page par hai, to robot automatically 
   us page par navigate (redirect) kare, page load hone ka wait kare, 
   phir us section tak scroll karke apna text dikhaye. Iske liye 
   sessionStorage/localStorage me current tour step number save karo taaki 
   naye page par load hone ke baad tour wahi se continue ho jahan chhoda tha.

4. Har section ko highlight karte waqt (blue border/glow ya spotlight 
   overlay effect se), scroll smooth ho aur box ka text us specific section 
   ke real content ke hisaab se accurate aur detailed ho — generic na ho.

5. Progress dikhane ke liye box me ek chota progress indicator bhi add 
   karo, jaise "Step 3 of 15" ya dots indicator, taaki user ko pata rahe 
   tour kitna baaki hai.

6. "Next" button ke saath ek "Previous/Back" button bhi add karo taaki 
   user peeche bhi ja sake agar wo kuch dobara sunna/padhna chahe.

7. Sabse aakhri step ke baad "Next" button "Finish Tour" ban jaye, click 
   karne par tour band ho jaye aur robot wapas minimized state me chala 
   jaye.

8. Sabse important: tour steps ka text content ek alag, easily-editable 
   config file/array me rakho (jaise tourConfig.js), taaki main baad me 
   sirf naye sections add karke ya text edit karke poora tour update kar 
   sakoon bina baaki logic/code chhede.

Is puri functionality ko current tour code ke andar hi implement karo, 
taaki robot ka design, position, aur styling same rahe — bas iski 
"intelligence" itni badha do ki wo pura website cover kare, sirf hero 
section pe na ruke.
